# Store questionnaire

These answers are for the App Store and Play Console forms. They describe this app.

- The app explains legal text that is loaded in the app. It is not a lawyer and it does not provide a professional relationship.
- Purchases are the Plus subscription: an auto-renewing monthly subscription on the stores, and Stripe on the web.
- The app works without an account. An account is optional and is used to keep notes and conversations when the person changes phones.
- The app does not contain advertising.
- The privacy policy and terms are the documents linked from Settings. A qualified person has not yet signed those drafts. Do not submit the listing until that review is named.
- Support address: the address shown in Settings.
- Screenshots for the listing must be taken from the release build's reader, search, and scenario screens.

TestFlight and Play internal testing have not been shipped from this repository state. There is no pilot outside the team yet.
